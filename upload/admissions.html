Entry Requirements

Ensure you meet the academic criteria for your chosen career path at SKTIM Madera.

National Certificates

2-Year Formal Programs examined by UVTAB/UBTEB.

  • UCE (O-Level) Certificate
  • Minimum 3 Credits in core subjects
  • UJTC/CPS equivalent accepted
  • Age: 16+ years

Vocational Skills

3-6 Months short courses for hands-on skills.

  • Open to all education levels
  • Primary (PLE) holders encouraged
  • No age limit for adult learners
  • Passion for the chosen trade

Required Documents

Keep these ready for your application process.

  • Original & Scanned Certificates
  • Valid National ID or Birth Cert
  • 3 Recent Passport Photos
  • Recommendation Letter
