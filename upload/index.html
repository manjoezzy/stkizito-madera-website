Welcome to St. Kizito's Technical Institute Madera

For over 75 years, St. Kizito's Technical Institute - Madera has been committed to providing quality technical and vocational education that empowers students with practical skills for the modern workforce. Founded in 1947 as a vernacular teacher training college by the Catholic Church, we evolved into a Rural Trade School in 1956 and attained technical institute status in 1986.

Our institute combines hands-on training with theoretical knowledge, preparing graduates to excel in their chosen fields and contribute meaningfully to their communities. We offer holistic technical education anchored on Christian principles while upholding good morals and co-curricular activities.
